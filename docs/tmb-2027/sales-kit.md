## 1. Sales page copy (for treksup.com/tmb-2027)

**Headline:** TMB 2027 bookings open on 15 October. Know exactly what to book first.

**Sub-headline:** A booking plan for independent hikers: which nights to lock in first, what to do when a refuge
is full, and reminders so you don't miss the windows. EUR 29, once. We don't book for you; you stay in control.

**Why now** (each point sourced, §5):
- Refuges in the Mon Tour du Mont-Blanc network open their 2027 bookings on **15 October 2026**. [S1]
- Booking day 2025 was rough: the new system crashed and people queued for hours. [S2]
- Some nights have very few beds (Les Chapieux, Trient). Missing them can mean a bus or taxi off the trail. [S3]
- Not every refuge is on the central platform. Some only take email or their own forms. [S4]
- From spring 2027, walking the French section without a reservation is set to end. The plan was agreed in
  principle in September 2026, with formal adoption expected later. [S5]

**What's in the plan** [confirm every bullet]:
- The order to book in: bottleneck nights first, then the flexible ones.
- A fallback for every stage, for when your first choice is full.
- Opening dates for refuges that aren't on the central platform.
- A reminder calendar: booking day, deposit and cancellation deadlines.

**What it is not:**
- It is not a booking service or a tour.
- It doesn't guarantee beds.
- It doesn't replace checking the official refuge sites.

**Price and alternatives** (honest comparison, §5):
- Free itinerary planners exist and are fine for splitting stages. [S6]
- DIY planners with live availability start at about EUR 20. [S7]
- Full planning packages and 1:1 consultations run EUR 79–200. [S8]
- This plan sits in between: EUR 29 for the booking-day part, which is the part people get wrong.

## 3. Free lead magnet: "15 October checklist" (one page, sourced)

1. Create your Mon Tour du Mont-Blanc account before 15 Oct. [S1]
2. Decide on direction and start date now. Book the bottleneck nights first (Les Chapieux, Trient). [S3]
3. List the refuges that are *not* on the central platform and email them on their own opening date. [S4]
4. Know the cancellation rule: more than 30 days before your stay you get 80% of the deposit back; later, nothing
   unless insured. [S11]
5. Keep one fallback per night (hotel or campsite, where the 2027 rules allow it). [S5]

Ends with: "Want the full order and fallbacks per stage? The TMB 2027 booking plan, EUR 29."
