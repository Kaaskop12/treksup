TMB 2027: the 15 October booking checklist

Refuge bookings for the Tour du Mont Blanc 2027 season open on 15 October 2026.[1] The busiest nights go fast. Five things to do, in order.

  1. Make your Mon Tour du Mont-Blanc account now. Most refuges and gîtes on the route are booked through that one platform.[1] Don't create it on booking morning.
  2. Pick your direction and start date before 15 October. Then book the nights with the fewest beds first, such as those around Les Chapieux and Trient,[3] and build the rest of your itinerary around them.
  3. List the refuges that are not on the platform. Some take bookings only by email or their own form.[4] Note each one's own opening date and write to them that day.
  4. Know the cancellation rule before you pay. Cancelling more than 30 days before your stay refunds 80% of the deposit; later, nothing unless you are insured.[5]
  5. Keep one fallback per night. From spring 2027, walking the French section continuously without a reservation is set to end (agreed in principle in September 2026).[2] Know which hotel, campsite or authorised bivouac you would use if a refuge is full.
