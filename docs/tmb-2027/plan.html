Why now

  • Refuges in the Mon Tour du Mont-Blanc network open their 2027 bookings on 15 October 2026.[1]
  • Some nights have very few beds, such as around Les Chapieux and Trient. Missing them can mean a bus or taxi off the trail.[3]
  • Not every refuge is on the central platform. Some only take email or their own forms.[4]
  • From spring 2027, walking the French section continuously without a reservation is set to end (agreed in principle in September 2026).[2]

What's in the plan

  • The order to book in: bottleneck nights first, then the flexible ones.
  • A fallback for every stage, for when your first choice is full.
  • Opening dates for refuges that aren't on the central platform.
  • A reminder calendar: booking day, deposit and cancellation deadlines.

What it is not

  • Not a booking service or a tour.
  • No guarantee of beds.
  • Not a replacement for checking the official refuge sites.
